RECOMMENDATION
This demonstration guidance is intentionally synthetic and is not a clinical guideline. It exists only to exercise retrieval and guard logic in a software smoke test.
Additional imaging evaluation can be represented for incomplete assessments. Short-interval follow-up can be represented for probably benign assessments. Biopsy language can be represented for suspicious assessments.

Routine screening can be represented for negative or benign assessments. Biopsy language can be represented for suspicious assessments and is not a substitute for clinical judgment.
For BI-RADS category 4, biopsy should be considered. For BI-RADS category 5, biopsy is strongly advised. BI-RADS categories 1 and 2 use routine screening, and category 3 uses short-interval follow-up.
